FinancePilot GPT — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

What the app accesses

With the owner's authorization, FinancePilot GPT has read-only access (the Google Sheets read-only scope) to a single spreadsheet owned by the same person: his Tiller Money workbook (Efrain_finances), which contains his own financial transactions.

How the data is used

The data is copied to the owner's own private server in his home and is used only for his personal finance tracking: budgets, bills, balances, and debt payoff.

Sharing

The data is never sold, never shared with third parties, never used for advertising, and never leaves systems the owner himself controls.

Storage and security

The data is stored on the owner's private server. Access runs through his Google authorization, which he can revoke at any time from his Google Account permissions page (myaccount.google.com, then Security, then Third-party access).

Contact

Questions can be directed to the app owner via the user support email shown on the app's Google consent screen.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address.
