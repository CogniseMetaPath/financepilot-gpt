FinancePilot GPT

FinancePilot GPT is a personal, single-user application, owned and operated by one individual for private use.

It synchronizes the owner's own Tiller Money spreadsheet (Efrain_finances) from Google Sheets to the owner's own private home server, where it supports his personal budgeting, bill tracking, and debt-payoff planning.

It is not offered to the public, has no other users, and is not a commercial product.
